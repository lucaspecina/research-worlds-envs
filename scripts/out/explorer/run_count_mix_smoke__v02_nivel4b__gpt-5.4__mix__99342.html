Razonamiento

REASONING: This strongly confirms a latent lot effect: within-lot variance (~5.1) is close to the mean, while between-lot variance is large and same-unit correlation is ~0.77. So a good structural model is likely Poisson measurements conditional on a lot-specific rate λ, with λ varying across lots, probably Gamma-distributed, which yields the NB marginal at speed 1.0 and positive repeat correlation. What remains is how speed scales λ: multiplicatively, and with what functional form. I'll run a small set of experiments at multiple speeds with repeats to estimate the mean-vs-speed relation and check whether the Gamma shape appears stable across speed.

Código

speeds = [0.8, 0.9, 1.1, 1.2]
rows = []
exps = {}
for sp in speeds:
    d = env.experiment(config={"speed": sp, "repeats_per_unit": 4}, n=120)
    exps[sp] = d
    g = d.groupby("unit_id")["y"]
    mu = d.y.mean()
    va = d.y.var(ddof=1)
    means = g.mean()
    vars_ = g.var()
    mean_within = vars_.mean()
    var_means = means.var(ddof=1)
    # method-of-moments gamma-poisson: Var(Y)=mu + mu^2/k
