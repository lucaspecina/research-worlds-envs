Razonamiento

REASONING: Repeated measurements are clearly not identical within unit; they look like fresh count draws with modest lot-to-lot variation. Since average within-unit variance is larger than variance of unit means, unit effects seem weak relative to measurement noise, so a simple Poisson or mildly overdispersed hierarchical count process may suffice. REASONING: The mean defect rate scales almost linearly with speed: mean/speed is roughly constant near 6.2, while mean/speed^2 declines, so a linear-in-speed Poisson rate is the leading hypothesis. Variance is close to mean across speeds, and within-unit heterogeneity looked weak, so a plain Poisson with rate lambda = alpha * speed may be enough.
