Razonamiento

REASONING: The speed effect is very close to proportional scaling of the latent rate: the edge means imply an exponent near 1, and within-unit variances also rise roughly with the mean, consistent with Poisson repeats. So a solid model is: each lot draws a latent baseline rate at speed 1 from some heterogeneous distribution, then each measurement for that lot is Poisson with rate lambda * speed. We have enough budget left to submit; using a gamma distribution for lot rates is a parsimonious continuous alternative to a finite mixture and matches the observed overdispersion plus strong repeat correlation. I'll estimate gamma parameters from the observed decomposition: if E[λ]=μ and Var(λ)=τ², then with Poisson repeats overall var across rows at speed 1 is μ + τ². From the baseline experiment, μ≈6.38125 and Var(unit means)≈Var(λ)+μ/4, so Var(λ)≈31.22656 - 6.38125/4.
